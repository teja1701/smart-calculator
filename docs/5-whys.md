PROBLEM : Customers leave the loan eligibility page without completing it.

WHY'S ????
1. Why are customers leaving the page?
   Because they find the process confusing.
2. Why is the process confusing?
   Because they aren't sure of information they need to enter.
3. Why don't they know what information to enter?
   Because the instructions are not clear.

Root Cause :
The loan eligibility page is difficult to understand because it does not have clear instructions.

Actionable Fix
Use simple instructions, improve the page design, and test it with users before releasing it.
